# ADR-009: Strict Inventory Consistency & Zero Overselling Guarantee

## Context
Flash sales experience massive contention on a single product's inventory record. Caches or optimistic counts cannot be trusted for authoritative stock checks.

## Decision
Inventory allocation relies strictly on relational SQL transactional invariants:
`available_quantity + reserved_quantity + sold_quantity == initial_stock`
And `available_quantity >= 0`.

## Rationale
Prevents financial loss, legal issues, and negative inventory states under 10,000 req/s concurrency.
